📚 Documentation & Methodology

SIH26103 — AI-Powered Predictive Analytics & Early Warning System

🎯 1. Problem Statement

IPMD, MoSPI monitors Central Sector Infrastructure Projects costing ₹150 crore and above. Under PAIMANA portal, 1,981 ongoing projects across 17 Ministries and 22 sectors are monitored.

Despite robust monitoring, projects face cost overruns, time overruns, milestone delays, contractual bottlenecks. Need: Move from descriptive to predictive & prescriptive monitoring using AI/ML/LLM.
